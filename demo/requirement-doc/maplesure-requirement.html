1. Business context

MapleSure sells group disability coverage to plan sponsors — employer organizations that sponsor coverage for their employees; the covered employees are members. When a member goes off work on disability, a claim must reach MapleSure's intake team. Today that submission is the plan sponsor's problem to solve, and it happens entirely outside SponsorConnect.

2. Current state

The current process is fragmented paper and PDF with limited visibility:

  • The member completes an employee statement; the sponsor completes a separate employer statement; an attending physician's statement is chased separately.
  • Forms are gathered by the sponsor's HR or benefits administrator over email, fax or post, then sent to MapleSure for manual intake and indexing.
  • Attachments arrive detached from the forms they belong to and are matched by hand.
  • Member and policy details are re-keyed by the sponsor on every form and re-keyed again by intake — transcription errors are a known rework driver.
  • The plan sponsor has no way to see whether a submission arrived, what is missing, or where it sits; status is answered by phone to the service desk.

3. Business objective

Give plan sponsors a guided online way to submit a disability claim for a member through SponsorConnect, and let them see that it arrived and what is still outstanding.

The business expects this to remove intake rework from incomplete or mismatched packets, cut status-chasing service-desk contacts, and create the first reliable measure of submission cycle time.

4. Requirements

  1. Identify the plan and member. The sponsor identifies whose claim this is from the policy number and member id they already hold.
  2. Pre-populate what MapleSure already knows. Member and plan details held against that policy and member id are shown rather than re-keyed; the sponsor confirms or corrects them.
  3. Collect the disability claim details. A guided journey collects employment and absence information, including last day worked and first day absent.
  4. Support multiple document uploads. Employer, employee and physician statements and supporting documents attach to the one submission, within the existing file type and size policy.
  5. Confirm receipt. The sponsor receives an on-screen confirmation and a submission reference at the moment of submission.
  6. Expose submission status. The sponsor can return later and see where the submission sits and what remains outstanding.
  7. Hand off to intake as one packet. The completed submission and its documents reach intake/indexing as a single associated packet rather than loose documents.

5. Out of scope

  • Adjudication of the claim itself, and any change to adjudication systems.
  • Member-initiated (direct-to-member) submission journeys.
  • Changes to the policy/member system of record beyond the lookup this journey consumes.
